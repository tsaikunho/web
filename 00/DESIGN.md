## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, technological refinement, and surgical precision for premium consumer tech hardware and ecosystem software. Drawing deeply from industrial design minimalism and modern human interface principles, the interface stays entirely out of the user's way, serving as an optically balanced stage for high-fidelity product imagery, cinematic typography, and tactile interactions.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Surgical Precision:** Every alignment, radius, and hairline edge feels mathematically resolved.
- **Expansive Calm:** Immense negative space allows hero hardware and editorial copy to breathe without distraction.
- **Tactile Modernism:** Transitions rely on realistic physical inertia, soft translucent materials, and gentle atmospheric light rather than harsh structural divisions.

### Movement & Aesthetic Lineage
The system synthesizes pure international minimalism with sophisticated translucent glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), high-density typography, and deep obsidian monochrome tones accented by purposeful electric sapphire blue.

## Colors

The palette operates on a disciplined scale where pure and tinted whites form the canvas, layered with calibrated obsidian shades and a solitary communicative accent.

### Color Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`):** Pure white canvas providing maximum contrast and brightness.
- **Canvas Secondary (`#f5f5f7`):** Soft, neutral off-white surface tone dedicated to structural cards, inset groupings, and hardware display trays.
- **Canvas Tertiary (`#fbfbfd`):** Ultra-subtle alternate background for alternating visual rhythms in long-scroll showcases.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#0071e3`):** The signature vibrant dynamic blue reserved strictly for actionable touchpoints, text links, interactive pills, and primary CTAs. An active/pressed state scales to `#0077ed`.
- **Primary Text & Obsidian Core (`#1d1d1f`):** The foundational ink value. Warmer and deeper than harsh synthetic black, delivering unmatched legibility for headline copy.
- **Secondary Gray (`#6e6e73`):** Standard secondary label tone for sub-copy, specs, supporting descriptors, and disabled states.
- **Muted Gray (`#86868b`):** De-emphasized metadata, legal disclaimers, and tertiary iconography.
- **Structural Hairline Border (`#d2d2d7`):** Precision 1px outline for cards, segmented dividers, and hardware swatch boundaries. Translucent equivalent: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Frosted Chrome (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`):** Dynamic navigation layer with `backdrop-filter: saturate(180%) blur(20px)` for floating navbars and action sheets.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes `Inter` tuned to reflect the geometric discipline, optical balance, and tight negative tracking characteristic of modern high-end operating systems. 

### Typographic Guidelines
- **Negative Tracking on Scale:** As font size increases, tracking tightens progressively from `-0.01em` up to `-0.035em` for massive display titles, preventing optical fragmentation and keeping headlines taut and punchy.
- **Eyebrows & Kicker Titles:** Small caps and uppercase categories use `eyebrow` styling (`12px`, `font-weight: 600`, letter-spacing: `0.06em`) in `#6e6e73` or `#0071e3` to announce sections before striking headlines.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Always pair strong `display-hero` or `headline-xl` titles with muted, spacious `body-xl` paragraphs in `#6e6e73` for high-end editorial product pacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is built on disciplined horizontal alignment contrasted with expansive, rhythmic vertical breath.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column layout maxing out at `1200px` for dense utility dashboards or `1440px` for cinematic hardware showpieces. Outer margins: `margin-desktop` (`4rem`). Gutters: `gutter-lg` (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Outer margins: `margin-tablet` (`2.5rem`). Gutters: `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Outer margins: `margin` (`1.5rem`). Gutters: `gutter-sm` (`1rem`).

### Float and Clearfix Foundation
While flex and grid containers handle modern responsive modules, the core promotional compositions leverage floating hero anchors and clean clearing blocks to allow fluid editorial wrap-arounds for hardware specifications, badge chips, and asymmetrical product photography. All float parents must implement standard pseudo-element clearing (`::after { content: ""; display: table; clear: both; }`).

### Vertical Rhythm
- **Hero & Key Section Separation:** Employ `space-3xl` (`6rem`) to `space-4xl` (`8rem`) between narrative acts.
- **Component Stacking:** Use `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) within cards and form groups to preserve a lightweight, uncluttered scan path.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is attained through a synthesis of surface luminosity, hairline demarcation, and diffused, ultra-low-opacity ambient drop shadows. Heavy drop shadows are strictly avoided; depth must feel like gentle atmospheric ambient light cast onto soft architectural surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure `#ffffff` or clean `#f5f5f7` full-bleed backgrounds.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Cards):** Solid `#ffffff` elevated upon `#f5f5f7` canvas, or `#f5f5f7` containers placed on white. Bound by a crisp `1px` border of `#d2d2d7` or `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Translucent Chrome):** Top headers, floating navigation pills, and interactive filter segments. Styled with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`, and a bottom or surrounding hairline border of `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays & Drawers):** Elevated dialogue containers with atmospheric shadow:
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
  - Outlined with crisp `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 4 (Hover / Active Interactivity):** Interactive product cards lift smoothly by `translateY(-2px)` accompanied by an expansion to `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is grounded in refined squircle-like curves and smooth pill radii that feel directly derived from modern consumer hardware casing.

### Corner Radius System
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Used universally for action buttons (CTAs), search pills, tag chips, segmented controller selectors, and floating top/bottom bars.
- **Hardware & Hero Cards (`18px – 24px`):** Large interactive cards, showcase banners, and modular grid cells employ smooth `1.25rem` (`20px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`) radii, emulating the physical corner radii of portable tech screens.
- **Standard Inputs & Flyouts (`10px – 12px`):** Text fields, menu dropdowns, tooltips, and nested components use `0.625rem` (`10px`) or `0.75rem` (`12px`) for a compact, neat appearance.
- **Color Swatches & Spec Glyphs (`50%`):** Perfect circles for product finish indicators (e.g., Space Gray, Starlight, Midnight, Silver).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary CTA:** Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`). Solid background `#0071e3`, typography `label-md` in pure `#ffffff`. Hover: `#0077ed`. Active: `#0062c4` with `transform: scale(0.98)`. Padding: `10px 22px`.
- **Secondary Action Button:** Rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`). Background `rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.08)`, text color `#0071e3`. Hover: `rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.15)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Pure typography with trailing arrow glyph (`Buy ›` or `Learn more ›`). Color `#0071e3`, hover underline or subtle `translateX(2px)` transition on arrow.
- **Disabled State:** Background `#f5f5f7`, text `#86868b`, border `none`, cursor `not-allowed`.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Segmented Control Container:** Pill container with background `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`, padding `3px`.
- **Segment Item:** Text `13px`, font-weight `500`. Selected item possesses an opaque `#ffffff` pill background, subtle shadow `0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`, and text `#1d1d1f`. Unselected items text `#6e6e73`.
- **Spec / Feature Chips:** Inset pill in `#f5f5f7` with `border: 1px solid #d2d2d7`, padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-sm` in `#1d1d1f`.

### Cards & Grid Containers
- **Product Showcase Card:** Built with `background: #f5f5f7`, `border-radius: 20px`, padding `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), and subtle border `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Typography inside aligns to left-top or center-top, leaving bottom space for uncropped, transparent product hardware PNG assets.
- **Interactive Specs Card:** High-contrast white card (`#ffffff`) over `#f5f5f7` track with hairline border `1px solid #d2d2d7` and hover lift transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, `border-radius: 12px`, border `1px solid #d2d2d7`, background `#ffffff`. Focused state transitions border to `#0071e3` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.2)` focus ring. Placeholder text styled with `#86868b`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` dimensions. Radios are circular (`50%`), Checkboxes have `5px` corner radius. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #86868b`. Checked: background `#0071e3` with crisp white vector checkmark or concentric inner dot.

### Lists & Tables
- **Specification Row:** Horizontal flex/float row separated by `1px solid #d2d2d7`. Left column spans `label-md` in `#86868b`, right column spans `body-md` in `#1d1d1f` with heavy weight for key performance metrics.
- **Navigation Lists:** Frictionless line items with hover reveal chevron and micro background illumination (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`).

### Hardware Color Selector (Custom Component)
- **Finish Swatches:** Array of `28px` circular color swatches (`Space Black`, `Natural Titanium`, `Starlight`).
- **State Behavior:** Active swatch shows an outer offset ring (`2px solid #0071e3`, `offset: 2px`).
